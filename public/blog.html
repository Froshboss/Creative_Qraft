Brand strategy

A clearer brand starts with better questions

A brand is more than a name or a visual style. It is the impression people carry away after interacting with your business. Starting with a few honest questions makes the creative work more focused:

  • Who do you want to make life easier for?
  • What do you do especially well for them?
  • What should someone understand or feel after meeting your brand?

Write down the answers in plain language. Those notes become a useful reference for your messaging, identity, website, and the decisions that follow.

Digital design

A more welcoming website is a more useful one

Accessibility is part of making a digital experience clear and comfortable to use. It is not a last-minute visual check. Start with foundations that help a wide range of people:

  • Use headings in a logical order and descriptive link text.
  • Make sure text has strong contrast and controls work with a keyboard.
  • Give meaningful images useful alternative text.
  • Test layouts on small screens and respect reduced-motion settings.

These details improve usability for people using assistive technology, browsing on a phone, or simply moving quickly through a page.

Creative practice

Make content consistency easier to maintain

Consistency does not mean repeating the same post. It means making it easy for people to recognise your voice wherever they meet you. A small content guide can help:

  • Choose a few topics your audience genuinely needs.
  • Write down the tone and words that feel true to your business.
  • Set basic rules for imagery, colour, and typography.
  • Use a realistic publishing rhythm you can sustain.

Clear guardrails give your team room to be creative while keeping every piece recognisably yours.
